Audit a short-answer assessment record with a five-way label.

Label meanings:
- correct: student answer fully expresses the required idea.
- partially_correct_incomplete: contains a correct relevant idea but omits an
  essential part.
- contradictory: directly conflicts with the reference or core science fact.
- irrelevant: does not answer the question or is unrelated.
- non_domain: cannot be meaningfully assessed in the expected subject domain.

Allow paraphrases, concise answers, and wording different from the reference.
Do not require exact lexical overlap. Judge whether the existing label is
reasonable; minor borderline ambiguity is acceptable.

When the record carries a passage (reading comprehension items such as RACE,
C3, TQA and WorldTree), judge the label against that passage: a student answer
is correct only if the passage supports it, and a reference that contradicts
the passage makes the record invalid.

behaviour itself is intact and usable; it is the critical validity check, so answer "no" when it is
broken, missing, unsafe, or unverifiable. Defects in the supervision that can be repaired must keep it "yes".
