OPEN FOUNDATION MODELS · EDUCATIONAL AI

Models that understand
how learning works.

OmniEdu is an open family of foundation models for K–12 learning and teaching. It connects curriculum grounding, reliable problem solving, learner diagnosis, and pedagogical tutoring in one capability-balanced training framework.

01 / CAPABILITIES

One family for the full learning–teaching loop.

01

Curriculum grounding

Map a question to knowledge points, grade levels, prerequisite relations, and curriculum evidence.

02

K–12 problem solving

Solve authentic school-level questions across subjects, formats, and multimodal inputs.

03

Diagnostic reasoning

Identify misconceptions, missing prerequisites, and knowledge-state gaps from learner work.

04

Pedagogical tutoring

Choose the next instructional action: a hint, question, prerequisite review, or explanation.

03 / DEPLOYMENT

Deploy in minutes.

Serve a released checkpoint locally with Transformers or expose an OpenAI-compatible endpoint through vLLM. The examples below use the 27B configuration; reduce tensor parallelism for smaller variants.

pip install -U vllm

vllm serve /path/to/OmniEdu-27B \\
  --served-model-name omniedu-27b \\
  --dtype bfloat16 \\
  --tensor-parallel-size 4 \\
  --max-model-len 32768
